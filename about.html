About Ridgeline

One shop. One standard. Every rifle earns its accuracy.

Ridgeline Precision Rifles is a custom rifle shop in Callao, Virginia, on the Northern Neck. The work here is builds and precision gunsmithing — hunting rifles, precision muzzleloaders, and competition rifles for PRS, NRL, and ELR — done one rifle at a time.

Every build starts with a conversation about what the rifle has to do. The components, machining, and setup follow from that answer, not from what's convenient to stock. That's why the shop runs by appointment: when you're here, the time is about your rifle and nobody else's.

Ridgeline is a federally licensed Type 07 firearms manufacturer, working with component lines from the makers serious shooters already trust — see the full list on the brands page.

If you're weighing a full custom build against upgrading a rifle you already own, call and talk it through. You'll get a straight answer either way, including when the honest answer is that you don't need the more expensive option.
