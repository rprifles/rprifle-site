Muzzleloaders that shoot like rifles

The early season is too good an opportunity to spend behind a factory smokepole. A properly built precision muzzleloader turns muzzleloader season into legitimate reach — with the same barrel quality and fit standards as any centerfire in the shop.

Muzzleloader season puts you in the woods before the pressure, but most factory muzzleloaders are the least accurate rifle a hunter owns. It doesn't have to be that way. A precision muzzleloader build gets a quality barrel, a proper stock or chassis fit, a real trigger, and an optic setup done to the same standard as a centerfire build.

The result is a rifle you can carry during the early season with honest confidence in shots that most muzzleloader hunters would pass on — and load work matters even more here than with centerfire, so development and testing support is part of the build.

These builds are specced around your state's legal requirements and your hunting style, so the first conversation covers where you hunt and what the seasons allow.
