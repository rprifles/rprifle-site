Custom Rifles · Hunting

Hunting rifles built for the field

A hunting rifle has one job: make the shot when it counts, after being carried, climbed with, rained on, and rested on whatever's handy. These builds balance accuracy against real-world weight and handling.

Call (804) 241-4949 Request This Build

A bench gun and a hunting gun are different animals. Weight you never notice at the range turns into regret on a long walk in, and a stock that feels fine off bags may not point worth a thing offhand. Hunting builds here start with how and where you hunt — stand hunting over Virginia hardwoods, still-hunting cutovers, or trips out west where shots stretch — and work backward to the spec.

That usually means a field-appropriate barrel contour instead of a heavy varmint tube, a stock that fits your length of pull and scope height, a cartridge matched to the game and realistic distances, and a trigger set where a cold-handed hunter can run it safely. Threaded muzzles for suppressors or brakes are standard practice if you want them.

If you already own a rifle that shoots almost well enough, a rebarrel, trigger, and proper optic setup through the gunsmithing side of the shop may get you there without a full build. Happy to talk through which path makes sense.
